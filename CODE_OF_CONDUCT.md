# 🧠 NEURAL SYNC – Code of Conduct

## 1. Our Commitment

NEURAL SYNC is committed to building an open, respectful, collaborative, and scientifically responsible research environment.

We aim to create a community where people from different backgrounds, disciplines, and levels of experience can work together toward ambitious technological goals.

Every contributor should feel welcome to participate, share ideas, ask questions, and challenge existing approaches.

## 2. Our Core Principles

All members of NEURAL SYNC are expected to uphold the following principles:

- **Respect:** Treat every contributor with dignity and respect.
- **Collaboration:** Encourage teamwork and knowledge sharing.
- **Scientific Integrity:** Value evidence, accuracy, and transparency.
- **Open-Mindedness:** Be willing to consider alternative perspectives.
- **Responsibility:** Consider the ethical and societal implications of our work.
- **Transparency:** Communicate honestly about progress, limitations, and mistakes.
- **Innovation:** Encourage experimentation and creative thinking.

## 3. Expected Behavior

We expect all community members to:

- Communicate professionally and respectfully.
- Provide constructive criticism.
- Welcome newcomers and support their learning.
- Respect different technical and scientific opinions.
- Give appropriate credit to other contributors.
- Share relevant knowledge and documentation.
- Discuss disagreements objectively.
- Protect confidential information and personal data.

## 4. Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation.
- Personal attacks and deliberate humiliation.
- Threats or abusive communication.
- Deliberate misinformation or falsification of research results.
- Plagiarism or misrepresentation of other people's work.
- Unauthorized disclosure of confidential information.
- Deliberate sabotage of project infrastructure.
- Misuse of project resources or contributor access.

## 5. Scientific and Research Responsibility

NEURAL SYNC operates at the intersection of artificial intelligence, neuroscience, EEG technology, brain-computer interfaces, and virtual reality.

Because these fields involve complex scientific and ethical challenges, all contributors must:

- Distinguish scientific evidence from speculation.
- Document research methods and experimental results.
- Respect human participants and their privacy.
- Obtain appropriate consent before collecting human data.
- Avoid unsupported medical or neurological claims.
- Respect applicable safety standards and regulations.

Scientific progress must never come at the expense of human dignity or safety.

## 6. Intellectual Property and Transparency

Contributors must respect the intellectual property of others.

All members should:

- Properly credit external sources.
- Respect software and research licenses.
- Avoid submitting unauthorized third-party material.
- Clearly identify externally developed components.
- Respect agreed project ownership and contribution policies.

Questions regarding ownership and licensing should be discussed transparently with project maintainers.

## 7. Handling Violations

If a community member violates this Code of Conduct, the project maintainers may take appropriate action.

Depending on the severity of the incident, actions may include:

1. A private warning.
2. Temporary restrictions on participation.
3. Removal of inappropriate content.
4. Suspension of repository access.
5. Permanent exclusion from the project community.

Serious violations may result in immediate action.

All reports should be handled fairly, confidentially where possible, and without retaliation against individuals who report concerns in good faith.

## 8. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all NEURAL SYNC project spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories.
- Discussions and issue trackers.
- Pull requests.
- Project communication channels.
- Official project events and meetings.
- Other spaces where individuals represent NEURAL SYNC.

## 9. Continuous Improvement

As NEURAL SYNC grows, this Code of Conduct may evolve.

Community members are encouraged to suggest improvements to ensure that our standards remain fair, transparent, and appropriate for an interdisciplinary research organization.

## Our Commitment to the Future

NEURAL SYNC is more than a software project.

It is a collaborative effort to explore the boundaries of human-computer interaction and investigate technologies that may shape the future.

We believe that ambitious scientific goals require not only technical excellence, but also integrity, responsibility, and mutual respect.
