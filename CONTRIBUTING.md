# 🤝 Contributing to NEURAL SYNC

Thank you for your interest in contributing to **NEURAL SYNC**!

We welcome developers, researchers, engineers, students, and enthusiasts who want to contribute to the exploration of artificial intelligence, neuroscience, brain-computer interfaces, and immersive virtual reality.

Our project is built on collaboration, scientific curiosity, and shared progress.

Every contribution matters.

---

## 🧠 1. Our Vision

NEURAL SYNC is a long-term interdisciplinary research and development initiative.

Our ultimate goal is to explore the scientific and technological foundations of **Full-Dive Virtual Reality**.

We approach this goal through parallel development of:

- Artificial Intelligence and Software Engineering
- EEG and Neural Signal Processing
- Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCI)
- Virtual Reality and Immersive Environments
- Experimental Research and Prototyping

We believe that meaningful progress requires collaboration across multiple disciplines.

---

## 👥 2. Who Can Contribute?

You do not need to be an experienced researcher or professional developer.

We welcome contributions from people with interests in:

| Field | Possible Contributions |
|---|---|
| Software Development | Programming, architecture, debugging |
| Artificial Intelligence | Machine learning, AI systems |
| Neuroscience | Research, literature reviews |
| EEG Technology | Signal acquisition and analysis |
| BCI Development | Interface concepts and prototypes |
| Virtual Reality | VR environments and interaction |
| Electronics | Hardware integration |
| Documentation | Technical writing and research documentation |

**Beginners are welcome.** Learning and developing together is part of our philosophy.

---

## 🚀 3. How to Get Started

If you want to contribute, follow these steps:

1. Explore the repository and read the README.md.
2. Review the current project roadmap.
3. Look through open issues and ongoing discussions.
4. Identify an area where you can contribute.
5. Open an issue to introduce your idea or proposed improvement.
6. Discuss your approach before starting major changes.
7. Submit your contribution through a Pull Request.

For larger contributions, please discuss the proposed work with the project maintainers first.

---

## 💻 4. Development Guidelines

We aim to maintain a clean, understandable, and reproducible codebase.

Please follow these principles:

- Write readable and maintainable code.
- Document important functions and systems.
- Keep changes focused and clearly explained.
- Avoid introducing unnecessary dependencies.
- Test your changes before submitting them.
- Never commit passwords, API keys, or private credentials.
- Document experimental results and limitations.

Whenever possible, provide reproducible examples.

---

## 🔬 5. Research Guidelines

Scientific integrity is essential to NEURAL SYNC.

Research contributions should:

- Include references to relevant scientific publications.
- Clearly distinguish established findings from hypotheses.
- Document experimental methods.
- Report limitations and uncertainties.
- Avoid presenting speculative results as proven facts.
- Respect privacy and ethical standards when working with human data.

EEG recordings and other potentially identifiable neural data must not be published without appropriate consent and privacy safeguards.

---

## 📬 6. Issues and Pull Requests

### Issues

Use GitHub Issues to:

- Report bugs.
- Suggest improvements.
- Propose research directions.
- Ask technical questions.
- Discuss new project ideas.

Please provide enough information for others to understand your proposal.

### Pull Requests

When submitting a Pull Request:

- Explain what you changed.
- Explain why the change is necessary.
- Reference related issues where applicable.
- Include testing information.
- Keep contributions focused and manageable.

All contributions are subject to review before integration.

---

## 🌍 7. Collaboration Principles

We expect everyone involved in NEURAL SYNC to respect the following principles:

1. Respect other contributors.
2. Encourage constructive discussions.
3. Share knowledge openly.
4. Accept technical criticism.
5. Support scientific transparency.
6. Respect different levels of experience.
7. Prioritize research quality over personal recognition.

We want to build an environment where ideas can be challenged and improved.

---

## 🔐 8. Intellectual Property and Contributions

Before contributing, please ensure that:

- You have the right to submit the code or material.
- Third-party licenses are respected.
- External research and publications are properly credited.
- No confidential or unauthorized data is included.

Contribution does not automatically transfer ownership of your intellectual property.

Specific ownership, licensing, and collaboration agreements may be established as the project develops.

---

## 🧭 9. Current Project Stage

NEURAL SYNC is currently in its initial development phase.

Our immediate priorities include:

- Establishing the software infrastructure.
- Developing the first functional software prototype.
- Exploring EEG hardware and signal acquisition.
- Building initial neural signal processing systems.
- Investigating AI integration.
- Establishing an interdisciplinary research team.

We encourage contributors to help shape these foundations.

We do not claim to have solved the challenges of Full-Dive VR. Instead, we want to investigate those challenges through systematic research, experimentation, and collaboration.

Whether you contribute code, scientific knowledge, hardware expertise, documentation, or ideas, you are welcome.
