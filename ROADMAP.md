# 🧠 NEURAL SYNC – Development Roadmap

### From Initial Research to Full-Dive Virtual Reality

**Project Status:** Initial Development  
**Project Type:** Interdisciplinary Research & Development  
**Long-Term Vision:** Full-Dive Virtual Reality

---

# 1. Project Vision

NEURAL SYNC aims to explore the scientific and technological foundations of advanced human-computer interaction.

Our long-term ambition is to investigate whether future technologies could enable deeply immersive virtual environments through direct neural interaction.

We approach this vision through parallel development of artificial intelligence, neuroscience, EEG technology, brain-computer interfaces, and virtual reality.

**Our objective is to turn ambitious research questions into measurable technical progress.**

# 3. Parallel Development Strategy

NEURAL SYNC will pursue multiple research areas simultaneously.

| Research Area | Main Focus |
|---|---|
| Software | Infrastructure and architecture |
| EEG | Neural signal acquisition |
| AI | Data interpretation and learning |
| BCI | Neural interaction |
| VR | Immersive environments |
| Research | Scientific validation |

The objective is to develop these areas independently while ensuring that they can eventually work together.

---

# 4. Research Principles

All development activities should follow these principles:

- Scientific integrity.
- Reproducible experiments.
- Open-source collaboration where possible.
- Responsible use of AI.
- Protection of human participants.
- Transparent documentation.
- Evidence-based development.

# 7. Our Long-Term Commitment

We recognize that Full-Dive VR represents an enormous scientific and engineering challenge.

We do not assume that every research direction will succeed.

Instead, we commit to investigating possibilities, documenting limitations, learning from experiments, and developing technologies responsibly.

**Our mission is not to promise the impossible. It is to systematically explore what may become possible.**
